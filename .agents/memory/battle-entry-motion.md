---
name: Battle entry motion
description: The intended animation behavior for YC Battle detail pages.
---

Arena jitter, electricity, and sparks are a one-time entry cue when a visitor opens a battle from the battle board. Direct loads and settled detail pages should remain static, apart from small status indicators.

**Why:** Continuous motion made the arena feel distracting rather than intentional. The motion should reinforce the transition into a matchup without competing with the vote decision.

**How to apply:** Keep the animation brief, respect reduced-motion preferences, and avoid adding looping decorative effects to the battle detail screen.
