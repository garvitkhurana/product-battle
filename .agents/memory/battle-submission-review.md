---
name: Battle submission review
description: The publication policy for community-submitted YC Battle matchups.
---

“Add a Battle” collects a full YC-company-versus-rival matchup, but submissions enter review rather than publishing immediately. Pending matchups are not listed publicly and cannot accept votes.

**Why:** The public board needs credible, comparable matchups and must not become vulnerable to spam or unsupported company claims.

**How to apply:** Keep the submission flow authenticated, retain the full factual context for both sides, and only make a reviewed matchup public when an editor marks it active.
