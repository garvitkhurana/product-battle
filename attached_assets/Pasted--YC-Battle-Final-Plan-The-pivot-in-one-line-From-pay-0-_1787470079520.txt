# YC Battle — Final Plan

## The pivot in one line

**From:** pay $0.99 to move a bar on a company you don't own.
**To:** swipe free, get your taste DNA, and give every company a perception profile — then publish the map of the whole ecosystem.

Same aesthetic, same domain, same battles. The money moves off the user and onto the companies.

## Phase 1 — The engine (weeks 1–2)

**Swipe.** Full-screen A vs B, free, no account, no limit. Keyboard arrows on desktop. This is the whole product — everything else is output.

**Elo behind it.** Pairwise → live ranking. Store session fingerprint, rate-limit, log everything.

**Tag your companies** on 5 axes so profiles can be computed: infra↔consumer, challenger↔incumbent, craft↔scale, batch era, regulated↔pure software.

**Seed real data.** 200–300 friends and Twitter mutuals swiping before any public post. Nothing may ever display `0 VOTES`.

## Phase 2 — The share loop (weeks 2–3)

**Personal DNA card** at ~20 swipes. Every line a real number:

> THE INFRA CONTRARIAN · backed the smaller company 11/15 · 78% infra · rarest 6% · closest match: Render, Supabase, Deel

Show a confidence indicator. Let them keep swiping to sharpen it. One-tap share to X with the card as image.

**Dynamic OG images** per battle and per company. `/companies/linear` previews as a live perception card. This is non-negotiable — without it, nothing you post anywhere renders.

## Phase 3 — Company side (weeks 3–4)

**One-word prompt** after ~10 swipes. Threshold at **5+ independent submissions** before a word appears — auto-filters garbage, makes every visible word meaningful, gives you a clean answer when a company complains.

**Company pages:** word cloud + backer profile ("backed by craft-leaning infra people, 71% contrarian") + affinity graph ("people who back Linear also back Figma, Vercel, Raycast").

**Claim-your-page flow** so founders are participants, not targets.

## Phase 4 — The map (week 5)

Cluster every company by who backs them. One image: the YC ecosystem arranged by taste, with visible clusters and the unexpected bridges between them.

**This is the launch asset.** It's the only thing that works on all three platforms.

## Launch sequence

**Hacker News** — Tuesday–Thursday, 7–9am PT.

> `Show HN: I mapped the YC ecosystem from 2M taste comparisons`

First comment, posted immediately: methodology, how you handled ballot-stuffing and transitivity violations, where the data is weak, the non-affiliation note, and a CSV link. Volunteer the criticism before they find it — HN rewards that more than polish. Then sit in the thread six hours and answer everything, especially the hostile stuff. Never ask for upvotes.

**X** — the map goes out as a native image. Then DM founders their company page. They quote-tweet their own cluster position, and that's your distribution — no premium account required, because it isn't your account doing the work.

**LinkedIn** — native image, insight framing ("what 8,000 people believe about the YC ecosystem"), link in the **first comment** not the body. Tag 3–5 founders max.

## Money

Consumer side free forever. Sell to the subjects, not the audience:

- **Perception dashboard** — sentiment over time, word drift after a launch, competitor benchmarking
- **Sponsored/custom brackets** — a VC running its portfolio through the machine
- **Data licensing** — the affinity graph

Companies already pay agencies five figures for worse versions of this.

---

## The three ways this fails

**Fabricated profiles.** If someone screenshots two contradictory results from the same swipes, you're a toy. Show confidence honestly; suppress axes with thin data.

**Adversarial founders.** A cloud full of "overhyped" turns your best amplifiers into enemies. Threshold hard, cap negative display, ship the claim flow.

**Empty launch.** Volume is the product. Do not post publicly until the numbers are real.
